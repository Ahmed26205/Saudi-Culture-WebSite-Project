Saudi Traditions

Culture in Everyday Life

Saudi culture is built on faith, family and hospitality. Each region has unique customs, but Saudis share common values visible in greetings, clothing and celebrations.

Key Traditions Across the Kingdom

These sections highlight major traditions such as hospitality, greetings, clothing and celebrations.

Hospitality & Arabic Coffee

Hospitality is a core Saudi value. Guests are welcomed with Arabic coffee and dates.

  • Serving coffee as a sign of honor.
  • Making guests feel relaxed and welcome.
  • Preparing generous meals for visitors.

Greetings & Social Etiquette

Greetings often begin with warm expressions and asking about well-being.

  • Starting with “Assalamu Alaikum”.
  • Showing respect to elders.
  • Regular family visits.

Traditional Dress

Saudi clothing is modest and represents culture and identity.

  • Men: thobe, shemagh/ghutra, bisht.
  • Women: abaya with regional variations.
  • Worn proudly on national occasions.

Celebrations & Family Life

Weddings and Eid bring families together with joy and tradition.

  • Eid prayers and gatherings.
  • Giving “Eidiya”.
  • Henna nights before weddings.

More About Saudi Traditions

Traditions preserve identity and strengthen community bonds across generations.

Respect for Elders

One of the strongest cultural values in Saudi society.

  • Priority in greetings and seating.
  • Seeking advice in important matters.
  • Using respectful language.

Family Gatherings

Families meet frequently, especially on weekends and holidays.

  • Sharing meals.
  • Strengthening bonds.
  • Celebrating together.

Words, Phrases & Proverbs

Words

Key Cultural Words

  • Dallah – Arabic coffee pot.
  • Majlis – Guest sitting room.
  • Agal – Black cord for ghutra.
Phrases

Common Saudi Phrases

  • “Hayyak Allah” – Warm welcome.
  • “Tasharrafna” – Honored to meet you.
  • “Bayyid Allah wajhak” – Thank you.
Proverbs

Saudi Proverbs

  • “Aljār qabl al-dār” – Choose neighbor before home.
  • “Man jarrab al-mujarab...” – Repeating mistakes leads to regret.
